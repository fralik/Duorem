Duorem has been modernized for Android 10 and newer.

- Added system light/dark theme support and an in-app help section.
- Added configurable home-screen widgets with device status updates.
- Added safer SSH host-key verification and encrypted saved credentials.
- Improved LAN discovery, Wake-on-LAN, SSH reliability, and error reporting.
- Added optional NetBIOS and root-assisted MAC address lookup.
